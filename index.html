PROJECT 01

Mission Mimosa — A Sense of Touch

Exploring low-cost visuo-tactile manipulation with a robotic arm by combining visual observations with tactile feedback.

The project brings together tactile sensing, robot learning and real-world manipulation, investigating how access to touch changes the way robots learn and interact with objects.

Through experiments with vision-only and vision-tactile setups, I'm studying what a robot gains when it can actually sense contact.
